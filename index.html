About me

I am computer science lecturer at GIKI.
My research focuses on Natural Language Processing (NLP), Large Language Models (LLMs), and Agentic AI, with an interest in developing trustworthy, human-centered AI systems. I investigate how language models and intelligent agents understand, retrieve, and utilize knowledge in multilingual and real-world settings, including multilingual/low-resource NLP, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and information retrieval and knowledge grounding.
I was awarded Outstanding Instructor by the Data Science Department at GIKI in May 2025. Current research focus on human-centered Multi Agent Systems and Responsible AI
