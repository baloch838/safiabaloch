Hello, I'm Safia.

I am a Lecturer at GIK Institute of Engineering Sciences and Technology, where I teach Data Science, Cloud Computing, Information Retrieval, and Big Data Analytics. I received my M.S. in Computer Science from FAST NUCES Karachi, where I also taught for over three years.

My research focuses on Natural Language Processing (NLP), Large Language Models (LLMs), and Agentic AI, with an interest in developing trustworthy, human-centered AI systems. I investigate how language models and intelligent agents understand, retrieve, and utilize knowledge in multilingual and real-world settings, including multilingual/low-resource NLP, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and information retrieval and knowledge grounding.

I was awarded Outstanding Instructor by the Data Science Department at GIKI in May 2025, and I served as an instructor at the ML to LLM Summer Bootcamp in the same year.
